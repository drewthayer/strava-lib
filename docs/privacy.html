Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Strava-lib ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal, non-commercial project built and maintained by Drew Thayer. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it's used, and your choices.

What data we access

When you connect your Strava account, you authorize Strava-lib to access certain data through Strava's official API, limited to the scopes you approve during login (for example: your profile info and activity data). We only request the scopes the app actually needs to function.

How we use it

We use this data solely to provide the app's core function: (e.g. "letting you view and download your own Strava activities"). We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

Where it's stored

  • Nothing is stored server-side: Strava-lib does not store your Strava data on any server. Data is fetched from Strava's API and processed/downloaded directly to your own device in the same session.

Sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose your data to third parties, except where required by law.

Your control & deletion

You can revoke Strava-lib's access to your Strava account at any time from your Strava account settings, under "My Apps". Revoking access immediately stops the app from accessing your data. To request deletion of any data we may have stored, contact us at Drew Thayer.

Children's privacy

Strava-lib is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
